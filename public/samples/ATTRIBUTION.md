# Sample product photos

The images in this folder come from the **Fashion Product Images** dataset by Param Aggarwal
(Kaggle, released under the MIT licence), via the high-resolution Hugging Face mirror
[PestoRosso/lamoda-fashion-product-images](https://huggingface.co/datasets/PestoRosso/lamoda-fashion-product-images) (MIT).

They are resized to 600×800 WebP and tagged with this app's taxonomy by `scripts/fetch_samples.py`.
The original product title and dataset id of every photo are recorded in `src/lib/sampleCatalog.json`.
Brand names visible on products belong to their owners; the photos are used only as demo content.
